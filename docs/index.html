The stash

Park it now. Find it a time later.

Dentist, haircut, call the plumber: the things you need to fit in but haven’t found a time for. Keep them in your stash, then drag one onto a free gap in any day.

  • Drop it on Today, the week or any day of the month
  • Snaps to quarter hours and to the events either side
  • Plans changed? Drag an event back into the stash
  • Your stash is yours alone

Privacy

Private to your cluster. That’s the point.

Cluster is for the people you actually live and plan with. There’s no public profile, no feed, and no way for a stranger to find you.

Read the privacy policy

  • Only people you invite

    Everything in a cluster is seen only by its members. Other clusters see nothing at all.

  • Private stays private

    A calendar you keep to yourself shows only for you, and a direct message stays between the two people in it.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising and no third-party trackers. Nothing you put in Cluster is sold.

  • Checked by the database itself

    Who can see what is enforced on every request at the data layer, not just hidden in the app.

  • Thoughtful about kids

    Accounts are for ages 13 and up. Younger children are part of the family without needing an account.

  • Leave whenever you like

    Delete your account from Settings at any time. No emails to send, no hoops.

Questions

Good to know before you start

Who can see what’s in my cluster?

Only the people you invite. A calendar you keep to yourself shows only for you, and a direct message stays between the two people in it. Other clusters see nothing at all.

Do my kids need an account?

No. Accounts are for ages 13 and up; younger children are part of the family without one. Picture schedules give them their own day to follow, on a tablet in kiosk mode if you like.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes, both ways: a change in Cluster reaches Google within a minute. School and club calendar feeds stay up to date on their own.

Is there an app?

Cluster works in any browser today, on a phone or a computer. Android and iPhone apps are on the way.

What if I’m somewhere with no signal?

Lists keep working. Ticks show up for everyone as soon as you’re back online.

Can I leave?

Any time. Delete your account from Settings: no emails to send, no hoops.
